\section{All pre-specified secondary quantities}
\label{app:secondary}

{\raggedright
Table~\ref{tab:secondary} lists every row of \texttt{outputs/stage3/results\_secondary.csv} (S1 regression and classification; S2 test-set size, accuracy; S3 platform within $E$; S4 deadline year within $E$; S5 formal accuracy test at minimum compatible sizes). Table~\ref{tab:s5} gives the top-decile summary that the plan pairs with the reference-adjusted gap, and Table~\ref{tab:s6} the S6 tests without flagged competitions. All three are generated by \texttt{scripts/make\_appendix\_secondary.py} from the result tables. Estimate: median over competitions, a difference in medians (rows ``minus''), a Spearman correlation (rows ``Spearman''), or a proportion of competitions (rows ``above 0.10''). Intervals: 95\% bootstrap over competitions; ``--'' where none was computed (Spearman rows and counts). Shares of positive values and the summaries in Table~\ref{tab:s5} also have no intervals; these omissions depart from the plan's general promise of intervals. Units: drift in the metric's own units (accuracy as a fraction); relative quantities in team spreads; accuracy gaps as fractions (0.01 is one percentage point). $n$: competitions, or the two groups' sizes; relative quantities exclude competitions without a team spread, so their $n$ can be smaller than for raw drift. Share: share of competitions with a positive value. $p$: Mann--Whitney for differences, Spearman test for correlations. Not confirmatory; not corrected for multiplicity.\par}
